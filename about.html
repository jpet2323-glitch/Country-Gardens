Our Story

Rooted in Robbinsville since 1986.

A family business on Robbinsville-Edinburg Road — and a neighborhood stop for 40 years.

Where It Started

A garden center that grew with its neighbors

Country Gardens opened in 1986 as a garden center on Robbinsville-Edinburg Road. 40 years later, we're still family owned and operated — and still the kind of place where you'll see the same faces behind the counter and the same neighbors coming back week after week.

Over the years, our customers kept asking for a little more, and we kept growing to meet them. Today Country Gardens is a farm market, deli, bakery, catering kitchen and garden center all in one — but the idea behind it hasn't changed: treat every customer like a neighbor, because they are.

What You'll Find Today

Made here, grown nearby, served by family

The Deli
Made-to-order Boar's Head sandwiches on Brooklyn bread baked in house daily, plus house-made soups and salads.
The Bakery
Our 10" deep-dish pies and famous apple cider donuts — available all year long.
The Farm Market
Jersey Fresh produce and homemade quiche, alongside local favorites like Terhune Orchards, Griggstown Farm, Arctic Ice Cream and local honey.
The Garden Center
Where it all began — seasonal flowers, plants and shrubs, and Christmas trees starting every mid-November.
Part of the Community

Proud to be your neighbors

For more than 30 years, we've helped local schools, teams and nonprofits raise money with our pies, hoagies, flowers and holiday wreaths. We love being part of the moments that matter here — from Thanksgiving tables and holiday parties to Friday lunch runs and first spring plantings.
